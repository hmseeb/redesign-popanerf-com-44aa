FAQ

Questions, answered

How long does an event last?

Every standard event package includes up to 2 hours of fully hosted gameplay — two full hours inside a real, enclosed battlefield with no downtime.

What is included with a booking?

A complete mobile Nerf battle arena set up at your location, a wide selection of blasters for all players, safety eye protection for every participant, a high-volume dart supply, team battle equipment including flags and game props, strategically placed battle barriers, and a dedicated Nerf Battle Referee who runs all games from start to finish.

Where can you set up the arena?

Inside or outside — home, park, playground, parking lot, or office. We transform almost any environment into a fully enclosed, battle-ready Nerf war zone.

How is pricing determined?

Pricing is based on group size so every event delivers a full-scale battle experience. Bookings start at $595.00 for 1 to 15 participants (minimum booking) and scale up to $995.00 for 31 to 35 participants. As your group grows, we expand the arena, increase equipment, and adjust staffing to match.

What if we have more than 35 players?

Groups of 36 or more participants require special pricing. Call 786-671-NERF (6373) and we'll build a plan around your group.

Do you host corporate and team-building events?

Yes. From birthday parties to corporate team-building events, parks, youth groups and office locations, every experience is designed to deliver a fully immersive, unforgettable Nerf battle.

Can we add on to the standard package?

Absolutely. Upgrades include extended ammo supply, an enhanced battlefield layout, tactical, Mega and Rival blasters, themed battle scenarios, water blasters, Night Ops missions, a shooting gallery, grab bags, tables and chairs, and Domino's Pizza catering.
